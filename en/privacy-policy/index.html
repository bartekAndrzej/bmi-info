This page explains what information BMI Info processes, why, and what rights you have. Last updated: 11 October 2026.

1. Data controller

The controller of your data is [name or company name], address: [address], email: [email address]. For any privacy questions, contact [email address].

2. What data we process

The BMI calculator runs entirely in your browser. The height and weight you enter are not sent to our server, not stored, and not shared with anyone. We do not operate user accounts, do not collect email addresses, and have no contact forms.

Because the site requires no sign-up, we do not process personal data that you provide to us.

3. Cookies

The site does not set its own cookies and does not use analytics tools. If we add features that require cookies, such as visitor statistics or advertising, this policy will be updated and you will be able to give or withdraw consent through a settings banner.

4. Third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When a font is requested, your IP address and basic browser information may be sent to Google's servers. Details are in Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Our hosting provider (for example Cloudflare Pages or Netlify) may record standard server logs needed to deliver and protect the site. The provider processes these under its own privacy policy.

5. Legal basis and purpose

Technical data described in section 4 is processed on the basis of the controller's legitimate interest in keeping the site running and secure (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, have it erased, restrict its processing, receive it in a portable format, and object to processing. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (uodo.gov.pl), or with the authority in your own EU country.

7. Children

The site is intended for adults. The BMI calculator is not designed to assess the weight of children and teenagers.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the site's features change. The current version is always published at this address.
